=====================================================
CUSTOM PHOTOS DIRECTORY
=====================================================

To replace any photo on the website with your own, simply drop your image into this folder with the matching filename (or keep the same name and replace the file):

1. unlock-photo.jpg
   -> The couple photo inside the purple scalloped circle frame on the Passcode Unlock screen.

2. birthday-reveal-photo.png (or .jpg)
   -> The central couple photo on the "HAPPY BIRTHDAY" celebration screen (under the party hat).

3. gift1-filmstrip.png (or .jpg)
   -> The photobooth vertical film strip on Gift 1 ("it's your dayyy" calendar).

4. letter-photo.png (or .jpg)
   -> The framed couple photo with the purple ribbon on Gift 2 (Love Letter screen).

5. museum-photo-1.png (or .jpg)
   -> Polaroid 1 in the "museum of US" gallery (top-left).

6. museum-photo-2.png (or .jpg)
   -> Polaroid 2 in the "museum of US" gallery (lower-left).

7. museum-photo-3.png (or .jpg)
   -> Polaroid 3 in the "museum of US" gallery (center).

8. museum-photo-4.png (or .jpg)
   -> Polaroid 4 in the "museum of US" gallery (lower-right).

9. museum-photo-5.png (or .jpg)
   -> Polaroid 5 in the "museum of US" gallery (upper-right).

Note: You can use .jpg or .png format! If you use a different extension or filename, you can also easily update it in:
src/config/birthdayConfig.ts
